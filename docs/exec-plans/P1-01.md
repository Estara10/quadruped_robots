## Objective

Establish an evidence-backed, end-to-end contract for the deployed Agile, RA and Recovery artifacts, including their true source, dimensions, observation order, joint/action order, foot/contact order and final mapping to Go2 motor targets.

## Why

The controller/hardware chain uses FR, FL, RR, RL, while runtime code may remap policy tensors to a different order. The actual order expected by the deployed TorchScripts is `UNKNOWN`. Until resolved, locomotion success, RA behavior and Recovery performance cannot prove a correct ABS interface.

## Scope

- identify the deployed Agile Policy source, export path and hash;
- identify the deployed Recovery Policy source, export path and hash;
- identify the deployed RA model source, dataset/checkpoint and Agile binding where evidence exists;
- capture Isaac Gym runtime DOF names and rigid-body/foot names for the relevant training run;
- determine Agile and Recovery joint/action order;
- determine contact/feet order;
- trace whether ROS 2 remapping is required;
- establish 61-D Agile, 19-D RA and 49-D Recovery observation contracts;
- trace all 12 actions to final FR/FL/RR/RL motor targets;
- preserve unresolved provenance as explicit `UNKNOWN`.

## Out of Scope

- changing Agile, RA or Recovery algorithms;
- changing Eq.21/Eq.22, switching, thresholds, hold timing or path gains;
- retraining or fine-tuning any model;
- performance tuning or formal benchmark runs;
- real-robot RL/ABS execution;
- treating visual MuJoCo behavior as mapping proof.

- current deployment artifacts recorded in `artifacts/manifest.yaml`;
- access to local training/export outputs if they still exist;
- Isaac Gym environment only when needed to print authoritative runtime names;

## Stop Conditions

Stop and report BLOCKED rather than guessing if:

- the deployed model cannot be tied to any trustworthy source/export evidence;
- authoritative Isaac Gym runtime names cannot be produced or recovered;
- candidate model/checkpoint hashes disagree unexpectedly;
- a required mapping can only be tested on a real robot;
- existing user changes overlap the files needed for implementation and cannot be preserved safely;
- evidence conflicts between paper, training, export and runtime code.

Do not resolve a stop condition by deleting a remap, changing a threshold, retraining, or trying higher-speed locomotion.
